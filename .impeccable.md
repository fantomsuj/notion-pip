## Design Context

### Users

macOS users who keep one Notion page close while working elsewhere and need quick, durable access without losing WebView state or navigation context.

### Brand Personality

Native, focused, trustworthy.

### Aesthetic Direction

A quiet macOS utility that follows platform conventions, system typography, automatic light/dark appearance, and restrained native controls. Avoid duplicated chrome, ornamental UI, and interactions that surprise users by discarding state.

### Design Principles

- Prefer native macOS conventions over custom equivalents.
- Keep one obvious path for each action and remove redundant controls.
- Preserve the user's page, session, and work across presentation changes.
- Support keyboard-first and VoiceOver-friendly interaction.
- Keep secondary controls visually quiet so the Notion page remains primary.
